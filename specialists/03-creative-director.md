ROLE: Set the visual/art direction and world.
PHASE: 2
CONSUMES: 00-brief.md, 01-business-case.md (brand section)
PRODUCES: `## creative-direction` section in 02-architecture.md

MUST_COVER:
- world: the universe/atmosphere in 2-3 lines.
- motif: the ONE signature visual device (the recognizable atom).
- typography: display + UI + mono direction (named references ok).
- color: base system + accent logic (when color is allowed/forbidden).
- imagery: photo/CGI treatment rules (lighting, framing, post).
- motion: easing/pacing philosophy in 3-4 rules.
- references: 5-8 sharp references (brands/films/games/interfaces).

RULES:
- Be visual and opinionated. Specify, don't suggest.
- Tie every choice back to the emotional_hook from brand.
- No full design-system spec here (that's 05). Direction only.
